# Startups

A venture-backed or bootstrapped software startup can start with about twenty
accounts. Three lines matter more here than in other industries:

- **SAFEs and convertible notes (26000):** investor money before a priced
  round. It is never revenue. Whether it sits as a liability or equity depends
  on its terms, so your accountant decides; the chart gives it its own line
  either way.
- **Deferred revenue (24000):** annual plans paid upfront are earned month by
  month. Without this line, the month a big annual contract is paid looks far
  better than it is.
- **Payroll split by team (60100, 60200):** makes research costs easy to find
  at year end for the research credit.

This folder uses the five-digit ranges from the ZeraBooks import layout
(10000s assets through 80000s other income); drop the last digit to line it up
with the other folders here.

Burn and runway come straight from the reconciled bank account: gross burn is
all cash out, net burn is cash out less customer payments, and runway is closing
cash divided by net burn. A worked month is in ZeraBooks' guide,
[Bookkeeping for startups](https://www.zerabooks.com/bookkeeping-for-startups).

The account names are generic and contain no real company data.
